TIDEMARK - BACKUP & RESTORE
Curling-wave logo assets

SELECTED DESIGN

This package contains concept 01, the curling wave. The vector artwork follows
the selected silhouette, including its return-arrow crest and teal lower wave.
The slight color variation in the generated concept has been replaced with
flat Kelp Software brand colors for consistent exports.

SOURCE AND REFERENCE

source/tidemark-wave-master.svg is the editable full-color vector master.
source/wave-paths.json contains the two editable outline paths.
source/palette.json contains the flat digital RGB color values.
reference/selected-concept.png is the original generated image for reference.

All logo SVGs use vector paths. They contain no embedded bitmap or font assets.
The symbol exports retain the same square canvas and padding as the app icon.
Keep their proportions when resizing. The opaque app-icon exports extend the
background to all four edges and leave corner shaping to the platform.
